Privacy Policy

Tisa ERP (web and mobile app) · Tisa Tech Secure · Last updated 27 October 2026

Tisa ERP is the internal business system of Tisa Tech Secure, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. It is used only by our employees and authorised staff. Accounts are created by our administrator; the app cannot be used by the public and has no sign-up.

What we collect

  • Account details – your name, work email, phone number, employee code, department and role.
  • Location – your precise location, only at the moment you check in or out for attendance, take a site photo, or upload a service report. The app does not track your location in the background.
  • Photos and files – attendance selfies, site photos, expense receipts, signatures and attachments you choose to take or upload.
  • Work records – tickets, service reports, tasks, messages with colleagues, expenses, leave and other records you create.
  • Device information – a push-notification token so we can send you work notifications, and basic technical logs (time, IP address) for security.

How we use it

Only to run our business: attendance and site-visit verification, assigning and completing work, approvals, billing, reimbursements, and keeping the system secure. We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use third-party tracking or analytics.

Who can see it

Your managers and the colleagues your role permits. Business documents (for example service reports or invoices) may be shared with the relevant client. Service providers who process data for us: our hosting provider (Railway) and push-notification delivery (Expo, Apple, Google). Data may also be disclosed where required by law.

Security and retention

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, passwords are stored hashed, and access is limited by role. We keep records for as long as you work with us and afterwards for as long as Indian tax, GST and employment laws require. Business and accounting records cannot be deleted before that period.

Your choices and rights

You can deny location, camera or notification permission in your phone settings; some features (such as attendance check-in) will then not work. You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. To delete your account, see Account deletion.
