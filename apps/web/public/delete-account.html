Delete your Tisa ERP account

Tisa ERP accounts belong to employees and staff of Tisa Tech Secure and are created by our administrator.

In the app, go to More → Delete my account, which opens this page. To delete your account and personal data, email info@tisa.in from your registered email address (or ask your manager). We will deactivate your login immediately and delete your profile details, push token, attendance selfies and personal messages within 30 days.

Business records you created (invoices, delivery challans, service reports, expense claims and approvals) are kept for the period required by Indian tax and GST law, but they are moved to a generic “Archive (former staff)” user so they no longer show your name.
