Common questions

How do I get an account?
Accounts are created by our administrator. Ask your manager or HR; you will receive your login email and a temporary password.

I forgot my password.
Ask your administrator to reset it. You will be asked to set a new password at your next sign-in.

Attendance check-in does not work.
Allow Location and Camera for Tisa ERP in your phone's Settings, turn on location services, and try again with a working internet connection.

I am not getting notifications.
Allow notifications for Tisa ERP in your phone's Settings, then sign out and sign in again.

Can I use the web version?
Yes – sign in at erp.tisa.in with the same account.
